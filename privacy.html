Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

ReelOrder (reelorder.com) is a free site with interactive timeline maps of movie and TV franchises. This page explains what we collect when you use it and what we do with it.

What we collect

You can browse every timeline without an account. If you sign in — with Google, Facebook, or an email magic link — we receive your basic profile from that provider: a display name, an avatar image, an email address, and the provider's user ID. That is all we ask for. We do not read your posts, timeline, friends, or contacts, and we never post on your behalf.

When you vote or comment, we store those votes and comments alongside your account so you get credit for them.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use it

Your account is used only for identity: to sign you in, to show your name and avatar next to your contributions, and to stop duplicate votes. Your email is used to send you the magic link if you sign in that way and for essential account notices. We do not send marketing email.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Your public display name, avatar, votes, and comments are visible to other visitors. Data is processed by the services we run on — Supabase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting), and Resend (transactional email) — each under their own privacy terms. We share data with no one else, except where the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

We use a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and everything tied to it, email hello@reelorder.com and we will remove it. You can also revoke ReelOrder's access from your Google or Facebook account settings.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
