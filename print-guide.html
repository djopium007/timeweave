2 Which size should I print?

We've given you the largest file. Printing smaller is always fine — the printer just scales it down. Don't go bigger than the sizes above or the fine timeline text starts to soften.

Statement piece

24 × 36 in / A1 / 50 × 70 cm. Above a couch, bed or desk. Every film title stays readable from across the room.

Gallery wall

A2, 18 × 24 in or 12 × 18 in. Mix with other frames; the detail rewards a closer look.

Desk / shelf

A3, A4, 8 × 12 in or 5 × 7 in in a tabletop frame. Great as a gift.

3 Where to print

Print shop (best)

Officeworks, Snap, Kwik Kopy, Staples, FedEx Office. Put the file on a USB stick or upload online, ask for a poster print on matte or satin paper at your size. They'll trim it to the frame.

Online photo labs

Snapfish, Shutterfly, Vistaprint, Printique, Nations Photo Lab. Upload, pick "poster" or "photo print" at the matching ratio, and it arrives at your door.

At home

A4 / letter on any decent inkjet using heavier matte photo paper (200 gsm+). Pick "Fit to page" and the highest quality setting.

Turn off auto-enhance / auto-colour. Print kiosks and online labs often apply it by default; on a dark poster like this it lifts the blacks and shifts the colours. Choose "no corrections" / "print as is".
