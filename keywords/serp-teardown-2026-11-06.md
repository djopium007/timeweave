# SERP teardown - 2026-11-06 (Ahrefs SERP overview, US, organic top 10)

Covers: scream / halloween / insidious "movies in order" (Blue card xmcfe8), plus the five anime
"watch order" SERPs run for the DR go/no-go test (card x1k372). Companion to the 05 Nov
'conjuring movies in order' check.

## Pattern (same on all 8 SERPs)

- Position 1 is an AI Overview or a People Also Ask box. Position 2 is an IMDb list or a Reddit thread.
- Wikipedia's franchise article sits at 3-4 with 100-400 referring domains - it is the only genuinely strong page.
- Every SERP has at least one thin page in the top 10 with 0-3 referring domains (IMDb user lists, MAL
  "interest stacks", Fandom forum threads, a WordPress hobby blog). Thin pages rank; no one has a branching map.
- Page format that wins: "How to watch X in order" article with H2 per film, release-order AND chronological
  sections, and a short FAQ. Nobody does total runtime or where-to-stream well.

## Backlinks of the weakest ranking pages (outreach targets)

Pulled live dofollow, non-spam referring domains for three mid-strength ranking pages:

- odeon.co.uk scream page: 1 real RD (eurekster.com DR 54, no traffic). Its 67 RDs are almost all nofollow/syndication. Nothing to chase.
- ign.com insidious page: 16 RDs, all DR 0-37 scraper/aggregator sites except movies-popcorn.com (DR 9, ~416 visits/mo) - a small movie blog that links out to "in order" guides. Candidate.
- goodhousekeeping halloween page: 12 RDs - vice, aol, huffpost UK, grunge (syndication, not reachable), plus five Beasley radio-station sites (country1025, wror, rock929rocks, hot969boston, idobi; DR 37-55) that ran a "Halloween movies in order" seasonal post. Candidates for next October.

Conclusion: linking TO the weak pages is not where the links are. The realistic targets are the weak ranking
pages themselves (link swap / insert, Frey's first-$50-link pattern):

1. moviebase.app (DR 25) - ranks for both scream and halloween with 0 RDs. An app resource blog; likely to say yes to a swap.
2. scrmbl.com (DR 32) - ranks #5 for evangelion watch order. Small editorial site.
3. suggestionlabs.com (DR 4) - ranks #8 for demon slayer. Tiny; a link from it is cheap and topical.
4. movies-popcorn.com (DR 9, 416 visits) - already links out to IGN's "in order" guide.
5. tommygirard.wordpress.com - hobby film blog ranking #10 for scream; free "resource" link ask.
6. directv.com/insider and amctheatres.com/amc-scene - editorial sections that already publish watch-order content; paid insert ask, DR 82, Frey's $75-100 tier.
7. Beasley radio sites (country1025, wror, rock929rocks, hot969boston) - seasonal Halloween posts; pitch in September.

Costs this session: 5 anime SERPs ~2,980 units (first three without the type filter), 3 horror SERPs 858,
3 backlink pulls 470. Lesson: always pass type=organic,question,discussion,ai_overview on serp-overview -
the AI Overview sitelink rows otherwise triple the cost.
